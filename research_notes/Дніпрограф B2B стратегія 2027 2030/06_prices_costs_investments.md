## 1. Market SELLING prices (Kyiv online, Dnipro, marketplaces)

### Takeaway
The best benchmark is Vizitka.com (Kyiv-based, ships nationwide). Its price tables are complete and current, for example SRA3 digital print at 8.5 UAH/sheet, 1000 business cards for 749 UAH and an A4 saddle-stitched 12-page brochure at 76 UAH/copy for a run of 100. Dnipro wide-format prices fall into two tiers. Mid-market shops charge about 190–230 UAH/m² for banner and 220–265 UAH/m² for Oracal print. Discount shops start at about 129 UAH/m². Kyiv online prices for small finished banners and films are 2–3 times higher (about 400–650 UAH/m²). I found no published prices for folding boxes, wobblers or notebooks broken down by run length.

## 3. Running costs: clicks, electricity, generator

### Takeaway
I found no Ukrainian click price for the Ricoh Pro C7500. The only benchmark is a US dealer quote for the predecessor Pro C7210x at $0.035 per colour click. Electricity for small business on the universal service in Aug–Sep 2026 cost **6.60–7.42 UAH/kWh** (class 1) or **8.49–9.52 UAH/kWh** (class 2), excluding VAT. Diesel was about 98.4 UAH/L in early October 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Click price benchmark (Pro C7210x, predecessor model)**: colour $0.035 and mono $0.009 per click, fixed for 60 months, in a single undated dealer proposal ([irga.com proposal example](https://irga.com/example-of-a-copier-proposal-evaluation-in-a-larry-hunt-news), via search summary). A PrintPlanet user cites "0.03$ A3 colour" without clearly naming the model ([printplanet](https://printplanet.com/threads/ricoh-pro-c7500-vs-c7200x.293674/)).
- **Electricity components** (article updated 14 Aug 2026):
  - Small business on the universal service paid 6.60–7.42 UAH/kWh on voltage class 1 and 8.49–9.52 UAH/kWh on class 2, excluding VAT.
  - Example breakdown (Kirovohrad PUP, class 2, Sept 2026), in UAH/MWh excluding VAT: purchase 5,405.27 + transmission 928.45 + distribution 2,958.11 + supplier 223.46 = **9,515.29**.
