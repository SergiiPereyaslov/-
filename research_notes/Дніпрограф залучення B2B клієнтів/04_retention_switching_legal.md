# Утримання B2B-клієнтів, переключення постачальників друку та правові межі «переманювання» в Україні (для «Дніпрограф»)

_Status: PARTIAL DRAFT — research in progress (2026-10-13)._

## (a) Why B2B buyers leave / switch print suppliers

### Cited Findings (international)
- PrintBuyersOnline poll of 84 top print buyers: 50% end supplier relationships indirectly ("just avoid them and stop sending them bids"); in a similar poll 59% "often/sometimes" tell a printer it lost a job on price even when it isn't the real reason. Reasons listed: misunderstandings, personality conflicts with sales rep, wish to try a new supplier, a serious mistake (one may be enough), perceived lack of value, non-meaningful differentiation. Suzanne Morgan, PI World, 2007-12-01 — [PI World](http://www.piworld.com/article/suzanne-morgan-explores-reasons-why-print-buyers-leave-their-printer-83303/)
- NAPCO Research/Canon survey, 200+ print buyers (article 2020-11-23): 86% specify processes/brands; 70% say equipment brand is key factor; >2/3 prefer G7-certified providers; 81% prefer providers with online ordering tool; colour matching & consistency high priority for selecting AND keeping providers — [PI World](https://www.piworld.com/post/print-buyer-survey-reveals-key-factors-influencing-provider-selection/)
