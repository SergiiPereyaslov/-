# Дніпрограф: джерела лідів, тригери купівлі та 45 нових цільових компаній (Дніпро, станом на 13.10.2026)

Method note. The core of the target list comes from a live pull of the public robota.ua search API for Dnipro (cityId=4). It covered 35 keywords, from marketing roles to industry words such as кав'ярня, стоматологія, автосалон, готель, фітнес-клуб, приватна школа, інтернет-магазин and франшиза. The vacancies are dated 11.09–12.10.2026. Company cards and vacancy texts were downloaded from the same API, and each vacancy used below was confirmed with `cityName = Днепр`. The pull was supplemented with local media, catalogues and company sites. Vacancy URLs follow the pattern `https://robota.ua/company<notebookId>/vacancy<vacancyId>`, the same format the previous report used. Vacancies expire, so a link may stop working after the posting closes. All companies in §3 are **absent from report §9.5** (40 targets). Product hypotheses and decision-maker roles are **[Гіпотеза]** unless a vacancy text says otherwise. No personal contact data was collected.

## 1. Lead sources: where to build a list of B2B prospects in Dnipro, extraction method, cost, effort, lead quality

### Takeaway
The best ratio of cost to lead quality comes from free sources that carry a **dated intent signal**: robota.ua and work.ua vacancies, the Prozorro API, local food and retail media, and industry catalogues (doc.ua, AUTO.RIA, LUN/DIM.RIA/Bild.ua, education.ua, craftbeerclub). Paid registries (YouControl, Opendatabot) suit **verification** and KVED-based lists. They do not show who is buying now. The ЄДР open data on data.gov.ua is free, but its usefulness for KVED/address filtering in 2026 is questionable (see Gaps).

### Cited Findings

**A. Job sites (free, best "intent" signal)**
- The public robota.ua API answers without authorisation: `GET https://api.robota.ua/vacancy/search?keyWords=<kw>&cityId=4&page=N&count=40`. Dnipro is cityId=4. With `count=100+` the API returns the error `"Count is too big"`, so results have to be paged. Company card: `https://api.robota.ua/companies/<notebookId>`. Tested live on 12–13.10.2026 (this research). The vacancy link pattern is the one used in the previous report, e.g. [robota.ua/company724246/vacancy11299755](https://robota.ua/company724246/vacancy11299755).
- One 35-keyword pull for Dnipro returned roughly 200 unique employers, about a quarter of them military units, which are not B2B targets. Among the rest were 13 dental clinics, 6 auto dealers and car importers, 4 coffee shops, about 9 restaurant and delivery chains, 4 fitness studios, 4 hotels and about 25 online stores (this research; examples with links in §3).
- Vacancy texts often state the network size and the trigger directly. Examples: «Факультет-Фарм… 92 Аптеки в 11-ти областях» ([robota.ua](https://robota.ua/company927092/vacancy11357955)); «М'ясоЛюб… шукаємо керівника для запуску мережі пекарень… з нуля» ([robota.ua](https://robota.ua/company14380288/vacancy11338437)); TRUSHKINA STRETCHING has «двома філіями на Правому березі… та на Лівому» ([robota.ua](https://robota.ua/company6831520/vacancy10682799)).
- The Факультет-Фарм company card on robota.ua says «101 Аптек у 9-ти областях», while the current vacancy says 92 pharmacies in 11 regions. The two figures conflict, and the card is probably outdated ([robota.ua company card via API, nb927092](https://robota.ua/company927092/vacancy11357955)).

**B. Prozorro (free public API)**
- Working query (tested 13.10.2026): `POST https://prozorro.gov.ua/api/search/tenders?cpv[]=79800000-2&region=49-53&page=1` with body `{}` returned **total = 180** procurements for CPV 79800000-2 (printing services) in Dnipropetrovsk region (postal codes 49–53). Example records: Синельниківська міськрада, «Висвітлення діяльності…», UA-2026-01-13-008785-a, 76 500 грн; Павлоградський міський центр соцслужб, табличка, UA-2024-04-24-011064-a (this research, API response). - CPV codes worth monitoring: 22000000-0 (printed matter), 22460000-1 (advertising materials, catalogues), 22900000-9 (other printed matter), 79800000-2, 79810000-5, 79820000-8, 79822500-7, 79823000-9, 39294100-0 (information/promotional products). Code list taken from the previous session's query files. Prozorro participation fees are 17 to 1 700 грн per bid, depending on the procurement value ([epravda.com.ua, 2016 — may be outdated](https://epravda.com.ua/news/2016/03/19/586004/)).

### Inferences
- A practical stack for Дніпрограф at near-zero cost: (1) a weekly robota.ua/work.ua script, (2) a weekly Prozorro query, (3) RSS or Telegram feeds from 4–5 local media, (4) a one-off pull of vertical catalogues (dental clinics from doc.ua, dealers from AUTO.RIA, developers from Bild/DIM.RIA, private schools from ISUO), (5) Opendatabot at 320–480 грн/міс to check counterparties before giving credit terms. YouControl's annual tariff is not justified at the start.
- Robota.ua data is noisy: military units, remote online sales jobs and vacancies at sole traders (ФОП) should be filtered out (inference from the composition of the pull).

## 2. Buying triggers and how to monitor them in practice

### Takeaway
The strongest triggers for short-run POS and printing are: **a new outlet or network launch**, **a marketing or SMM hire**, **a category or trade-marketing manager hire**, **exhibition participation or an event**, **grants and loans for equipment**, and **a residential complex handover or sales start**. October 2026 vacancies in Dnipro show several of these directly.

### Inferences
- Marketing and category-manager vacancies give a **window of about 2–6 weeks**: a new hire looks for "their own" suppliers [Гіпотеза]. Getting in touch in the week the vacancy closes is the most valuable timing.
- Front-of-house hires (baristas, administrators) are a weak signal unless combined with a new address. Combined, they are strong.
